# LC Foods — ESG Data Integration & Supply Chain Digital Transformation

**Business Analysis · ESG Data Governance · ERP Solution Evaluation · Implementation Planning**

> **Project type:** University team project / academic product and business proposal  
> **Project stage:** Concept / analysis; not a deployed or commercially validated solution  
> **Industry:** Food processing, export supply chains & ESG

An academic enterprise business case exploring how ESG data can be integrated with SAP S/4HANA and a supplier portal to support traceability and export readiness.

## Business context
The case considers an integrated **Feed–Farm–Food (3F)** supply chain and the challenge of gathering non-financial ESG information alongside existing operational transaction records. The proposed initiative centers on export traceability for the Bento product line, supplier data onboarding and ESG evidence for reporting and potential green financing.

**Core problem:** ESG evidence is fragmented across people, processes and systems, making reliable end-to-end tracing and audit preparation difficult.

| Target | Proposed measure |
| --- | --- |
| Export-market growth | +25% export revenue for the Bento line |

## 1. Problem discovery & analysis
The analysis distinguishes three gaps:
- **Process:** inconsistent collection and no standardized audit-ready traceability flow across the chain.
- **People:** unclear ESG/data ownership and uneven supplier readiness.
- **Technology:** existing ERP transaction data does not automatically provide complete ESG provenance, batch evidence or supplier declarations.

The team prepared an AS-IS/GAP/TO-BE assessment before comparing solutions.

## 2. Proposed solution and decision-making
## Alternatives considered
| Option | Approach | Decision implication |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Maintain and improve manual reporting | Lower change burden but leaves traceability fragmentation |
| 2 | Blockchain pilot for Feed–Farm | Immutable-record concept but higher adoption/integration complexity |
| **3 — Recommended** | **SAP S/4HANA ESG integration + Supplier Portal** | Builds on existing ERP environment and is easier to introduce to suppliers via web/mobile |

**Recommendation in the case:** Option 3, assessed against costs, risks, stakeholders, feasibility, scalability and strategic fit. This is a recommended academic solution, **not confirmation of adoption by LC Foods**.

### Proposed solution components
1. SAP S/4HANA ESG fields for procurement, inventory and production data.
2. Supplier Portal for origin declarations and certification uploads.
3. QR-code batch traceability linking data to export product batches.
4. ESG dossier/reporting outputs for compliance review and financing evidence.

## 3. Evidence, validation and limits
The report provides an **evaluation framework**, not results from a completed live pilot. It compares three solution options and proposes a future pilot with **five strategic seafood suppliers**, one production line and **2–3 Bento export batches**. Proposed pilot goals include 90% supplier adoption, over 95% data accuracy, complete QR functionality and under 5% production disruption.

## 4. Strategy & implementation
The source proposes a phased rollout and change-management plan involving procurement, suppliers, QA/factory, IT/BA, finance and leadership.

**Business-case model:** Estimated total project cost **VND 25.98 billion**, estimated financial benefit **VND 51.1 billion**, and modeled ROI **96.69%**. These figures are projections based on the assignment's assumptions and are **not realized gains**.

## 5. Outcomes and learnings
This case demonstrates structured business problem definition, stakeholder mapping, AS-IS/TO-BE analysis, multi-criteria trade-off assessment, functional decomposition of an enterprise solution and phased pilot planning. No production deployment or verified business impact is claimed.

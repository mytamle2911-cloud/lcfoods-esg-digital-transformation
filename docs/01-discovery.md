# Problem, stakeholders and AS-IS / TO-BE

## Problem statement
The case argues that ESG evidence cannot be managed consistently through transaction-focused systems alone. Missing source documentation, packaging materials data and supplier inputs complicate export traceability and ESG reporting.

## Gap analysis
| Dimension | AS-IS in case | Gap | TO-BE proposal |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Process | Fragmented records | Lack of chain-wide evidence | Standardized collection and traceability |
| People | Distributed ownership | Inconsistent accountability | ESG/data roles and supplier enablement |
| Technology | ERP transactional focus | Missing ESG data layer | ESG-enriched SAP records and external supplier intake |

## Stakeholder groups
Leadership (sponsor), IT/BA (integration and requirements), procurement (supplier data), QA/factory (quality evidence), suppliers (declarations), export customers and financing institutions.

**Source:** Business Case §§3–4, physical PDF pages 9–14.

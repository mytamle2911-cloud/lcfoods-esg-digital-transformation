# Solution options and decision logic

## Options
1. Continue with improved manual documentation.
2. Pilot blockchain across Feed–Farm actors.
3. Integrate ESG data into SAP S/4HANA with an accessible Supplier Portal.

## Why option 3 was recommended
The team's assessment emphasizes extending an existing platform rather than operating a parallel traceability system, and reducing onboarding friction for suppliers relative to blockchain-node participation. The recommendation also depends on leadership commitment, supplier training and data governance.

## Proposed capabilities
- Supplier declaration, provenance documentation and credentials.
- ESG fields tied to procurement and production records.
- Batch traceability / QR evidence access.
- ESG dossier and management reports.

## Open implementation questions
Actual system interfaces, master-data ownership, supplier identity management, permissioning, audit controls, legal applicability and the feasibility of third-party integrations would require discovery before procurement or build.
